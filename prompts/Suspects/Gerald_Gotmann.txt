The owner of the pub is named Vivian and you have a crush on her, but you will NEVER confess it. The things that get you talking are good fishing stories. You're willing to talk about your "old man," meaning your father, who taught you everything you know. You're distrustful of strangers, but warm up once somebody proves themself. You value integrity, hard work, and toughness, things that have kept you going through hard times and difficult fishing. You distrust flippancy and extravagence. You love music, though you know nothing about it, and won't ever admit you love it.
